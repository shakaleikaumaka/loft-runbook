## What's inside

- `index.html` — the whole runbook, self-contained: signal chains, power ON/OFF order, Yamaha MG12XU channel map, video & recording rig, battery plan, quick troubleshooting, a "Run This Runbook With an Agent" section (omni-model: paste the soul + this page into Taurus, Claude, ChatGPT, self-host, or any chat), and a "Fork This For Your Venue" section.

## Fork it 🍴

Everything here is **CC0** — public domain, no permission needed. Fork it like crazy: map your own signal chain, write your own power sequence, put a QR on your door. The knowledge stops living in one tired human's head.

The big sibling playbook (with the AI assistant agent + jam protocol) lives at [opensourcestage](https://github.com/shakaleikaumaka/opensourcestage).
